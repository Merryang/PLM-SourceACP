Supplementary Table S4. Comparison on the original Aegis benchmark.

All rows refer to the original 138-sequence Aegis test partition (108 ACPs and 30 non-ACPs). Values for this study were recalculated from the archived three-seed ensemble probabilities and predictions. Its decision threshold was 0.4423, the median of the validation-selected seed thresholds.
