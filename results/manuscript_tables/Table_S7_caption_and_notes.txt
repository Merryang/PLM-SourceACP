Supplementary Table S7. Source-specific performance by sequence length.

Lengths are residue counts. LOSO values are means of seed-specific metrics for seeds 42, 2024 and 2026. Integrated-stack values use the fixed C = 3 predictor on overlapping source-membership subsets. Binary metrics use threshold 0.5. All Test2 sequences have at most 50 residues, so its All and 11–50 strata coincide. No model was refitted or reselected. Subgroups differ in membership and class composition, and overall AUC includes comparisons across length strata. These descriptive results do not estimate a causal length effect. LOSO, leave-one-source-out. ACP, anticancer peptide. AUC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. MCC, Matthews correlation coefficient. C, inverse regularization strength.
